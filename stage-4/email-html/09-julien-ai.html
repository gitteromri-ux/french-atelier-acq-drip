Sometimes the word you want arrives after class has finished. You remember the question you meant to ask, or want another attempt at a phrase you found difficult.

Julien is French Atelier by Acadomia's AI tutor for practice between live lessons. Available around the clock, it offers pronunciation coaching, corrections and conversation practice in French.

Keep the task small. You might practise introducing yourself, repeat a sentence from class or rehearse one question you want to ask on a trip. Choose something you can return to rather than a whole topic at once.

Julien is an AI practice companion, not your human teacher. Use the extra practice to identify what you want to work on, then bring any unresolved questions to class.

A short café exchange is one place to begin: familiar words, a cultural setting and another chance to speak.
