## Topic checks

- **01 Brittany and Breton:** Bretagne, Celtic connection, two-way language influence, local words, cultural facts.
- **02 Coco Chanel:** Specific fashion changes, cultural influence, French vocabulary, memorable facts; no invented-fashion claim.
- **03 Arriving at CDG:** Arrival sequence, passport control, baggage, signs, transport and asking for help.
- **04 Seven French dishes:** Two or three email teasers; seven ranked LP dishes, regions, texture and taste vocabulary.
- **05 Charlemagne:** Learning, administration, language, religion, fragmentation and cultural consolidation; no founder-of-France claim.
- **06 Strasbourg Christmas:** Cathedral, Alsace, lights, chalets, spices, treats and crafts; no fixed dates.
- **07 French café challenge:** Coffee scenario, simple phrases, discount and limited-time language without dates.
- **08 Final-chance promotion:** X% as supplied, few places in the next class, real teachers and cultural experience.
- **09 Julien AI:** Between-class practice, cultural enrichment and an approachable AI companion.
- **10 Courses:** Acquisition audience, current levels, course differences and cultural immersion.
- **11 CEFR-aligned certificate:** Course context, learning and cultural benefits, certificate on completion.
