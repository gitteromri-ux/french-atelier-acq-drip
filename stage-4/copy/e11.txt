Introducing yourself, understanding a familiar question or explaining what you would like: these are achievements worth noticing as you learn French.

French Atelier by Acadomia marks course completion with a CEFR-aligned certificate issued by Acadomia. Both The French Atelier and Let's Speak French collections connect language practice with cultural settings.

CEFR stands for Common European Framework of Reference for Languages. It describes language proficiency through what people can do, helping you put your progress into clearer terms.

Keep your own examples alongside that milestone. Which conversation feels easier? What can you understand that once needed a translation? Your answers make the learning personal.

Your certificate marks a completed course. Use it as a moment to recognise the work and choose what you want to learn next.
