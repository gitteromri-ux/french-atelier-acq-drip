Galette des Rois: the cake with a crown

Bonjour {{BODY_1}} 👑

A golden cake sits on the table, its puff pastry cut into slices. There is almond filling inside, but that is not the only thing people are hoping to find.

Around Epiphany in January, the galette des rois brings a game to the French table. La galette means cake here. Hidden inside is la fève, a small charm. Whoever finds it gets la couronne, the crown, and becomes king or queen for the day.

The crown is paper, but the suspense can feel real.

Here is the fun part: traditionally, the youngest child hides under the table and calls out who gets each slice. With the cake out of sight, the choice is left to chance.

A pause, a smile, and there is the charm on a plate. That is one way to make a January cake memorable.

Who gets the next slice?
