# French Atelier holiday delivery

## Included
Four educational emails and four distinct landing pages. Each pair includes subject, preheader, live headline, short CTA, individual TXT copy, structured JSON, browser preview and table-based email HTML. Twelve JPEGs use Chris's exact sizes: email 1200×700, desktop 1920×502, mobile 758×556.

## Image sources
Papillotes and Saint Nicolas: Artlist Nano Banana Pro, native downloaded output 5504×3072 each. Réveillon: Higgsfield generated output 5504×3072. These are generated editorial scenes, not documentary photographs. No enlargement or visible watermark was added.

Lyon: actual festival photograph, 4608×3072, by AlSepPhoenix. [Original photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Musee_Beaux_art_Lyon_Fete_Lumiere_2024.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The image depicts the Musée des Beaux-Arts at Place des Terreaux, not the Hôtel de Ville. Crops retain attribution and the same licence. The photographer's licence does not establish separate projected-artwork clearance; confirm that permission before campaign use. This is the remaining image-rights gate, not a resolution gap.

The Saint Nicolas desktop banner contains the photograph on a navy field to retain the whole mitre, staff, children and treat handover. It does not crop out the defining cultural details.

## Copy audit
Email/LP counts: Papillotes 108/275; Lumières 108/278; Saint Nicolas 112/299; Réveillon 119/299. The build checks the topic-specific ranges. Email counts exclude metadata, greeting, CTA and signoff; LP counts include subheads.

French Atelier's learning value is explicit without introducing a product or course offer. No selling, urgency, send/event dates, previous-email references or videos. The required historical year 1852 is retained. The Christmas social-promo site's Paris imagery was reviewed but not substituted for unrelated topics.
