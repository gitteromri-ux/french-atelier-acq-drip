Les Papillotes

Learn four French words at a Christmas table

Bonjour,

Learn a little French with French Atelier through a Christmas tradition from Lyon: les papillotes. These individually wrapped chocolates contain a printed joke, quotation or riddle, often read aloud when people share them.

Picture someone opening one at the table. Une papillote names the wrapped chocolate; un emballage means a wrapper. The paper inside carries un petit mot, a little message, or perhaps une devinette, a riddle.

Now try asking, « Qu’est-ce qui est écrit ? » It means “What does it say?” You have a question to use, not just a list to memorise.
