How French Atelier works

Learn French one region at a time.

A course that travels

French Atelier does not teach from a list of disconnected topics. It moves you through France itself, one region at a time, so the language always has a place attached to it 🗺️.

The structured collection, The French Atelier, holds four courses that carry you from A0 to A2.2 on the CEFR scale, the standard used across Europe to measure language level. Each course holds 20 lessons, and each one is set in a different part of France.

You begin in Paris, learning the French you would need on your first morning in the capital. From there you travel to Normandy, Paris, and Versailles, adding the vocabulary of coastline, court, and countryside. Next comes the Loire, Bordeaux, and the Basque Country, where the language shifts toward food, wine, and the southwest. The final course moves through Marseille, Chamonix, and Alsace, from Mediterranean streets to Alpine villages to the German-influenced east.

Each region brings its own useful phrases along with it. In the Basque Country, you might learn c'est délicieux, meaning this is delicious, ordering at a market stall. In Chamonix, you might learn quel est le sommet là-bas?, meaning what is that peak over there?

By the time you finish all four courses, you have not just studied grammar in the abstract. You have a working sense of how French sounds and shifts from the north coast to the Alps, the same way a real trip through the country would teach you.

For learners who want spoken fluency without grammar drills, Let's Speak French offers three shorter courses of 10 lessons each, built purely around conversation.
